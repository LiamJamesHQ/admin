Section 1

What we need from you, on one page

You already answer our phones and qualify callers every day. Wave 2 changes one thing: these leads come in through the system, and the system reacts to what you record. Four things, in this order, every time.

  • 📞
    Call every new Wave 2 lead as soon as it arrivesMonday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00. The lead is warm for minutes, not hours.
  • 💻
    Make the call from inside GHL, not a separate phoneThe system only knows a call happened if it went through GHL. A call made elsewhere is invisible to it.
  • 📝
    Record the outcome: a note, and one tagThe most important job after the call itself. Section 4 covers exactly how.
  • 📅
    Book warm leads into Book a Call BackThe booking itself moves the lead forward and sends confirmations. Section 5.
Why it mattersOnce you have logged the call, automation takes over: follow up messages, reminders, and alerts to the team all fire off what you recorded. If the record is wrong or missing, the customer gets the wrong message or none at all.

In short

  • Call within minutes, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 18:00
  • Calls made outside GHL do not exist to the system
  • After the call: one note, one tag
  • Warm leads go into Book a Call Back

Moving a lead by hand

You can drag a lead between stages, and there will be moments when you should. Two things to know first: a hand move can be overwritten a few minutes later when the automation writes its own outcome, and some stages fire messages when a lead lands in them. So the rule is: log the outcome and let the system move it, and only move by hand when the stage is plainly wrong and you have already left a note saying why.

For discussion with LiamWhich single case, if any, is a hand move always allowed in. Suggested: "system placed them in the wrong stage and the note explains why".

In short

  • Your stages: New Lead and Call Booked with Closer
  • Closer stages: Estimate Sent and Deposit Paid
  • Everything in between moves off your call record
  • Hand moves only when the stage is plainly wrong, with a note first

Section 3

Four outcomes, and what each one sets off

Every completed call lands in one of these. Your note and tag decide which. Here is what happens next in each case, so you can tell a caller what to expect.

🔥 Connected, warm

You do
Book them into Book a Call Back there and then if they will take a slot. If not, note and tag warm.
Then
If booked: confirmation, reminders and closer notification fire. If not booked: a WhatsApp with the invitation to visit the workshop, then a gentle check in sequence from Marta over the following days.
Never
Give a price or an estimate. The closer does that on the booked call.

❄️ Connected, cold

You do
Note the reason they are not interested. Tag cold.
Then
One light cross sell message showing the wider range, then the system leaves them alone.
Never
Push. A clear "no thanks" is a fine outcome and a clean record.

📵 No answer or voicemail

You do
Let the call finish so GHL records it. Note "no answer" or "voicemail left". Do not call again yourself yet.
Then
The system runs a timed chase: messages spaced out over the following days inviting them to pick a time. If they reply, you will be alerted to call again.
Never
Ring three times in a row. The chase is designed so one attempt is enough from you.

🤔 Connected, unclear

You do
Write the fullest note of the four. What they asked, what you could not answer, what they want next. Tag human follow up needed.
Then
If nothing has moved in 48 hours, an alert goes to Nikki, who reads your note and routes it.
Never
Leave it with no note. A blank unclear is the one outcome the system cannot recover.
How the system reads your callAfter every call, an automation reads the call and your note and writes an outcome. A clear note in plain words ("wants a 6 person sauna for a garden, asked about delivery to Devon, happy to book Thursday") makes that reading accurate. A one word note makes it guess.

In short

  • Warm: book the slot, or tag warm and the workshop invite goes out
  • Cold: note the reason, tag cold, one cross sell then silence
  • No answer: let it ring out, note it, the chase takes over
  • Unclear: fullest note, tag human follow up needed, Nikki picks it up in 48 hours
  • Price and lead times are the closer's

Section 4

Tags and notes: the job that makes everything else work

Tags are how the rest of the team and the automation know a lead's current status without listening to the call. Clean tags are the difference between a customer getting the right message and the wrong one.

Two kinds of tag

Some tags are placed by the system. Leave those alone, even if one looks wrong. The others are yours to manage.

wave 2 facebook/meta leadwave 2 no answer chase startedno answer chase exhaustedwave 2 call bookedcloser call outcome is ...

System tags, shaded green.

🔥 warm❄️ cold📵 no answer💬 currently in conversation🙋 human follow up needed

Your tags, honey outline. Exact spellings to be confirmed, see Open decisions.

The rules

  • 1️⃣
    One tag per call.The tag you leave is the single most important thing about that call: the status, or the action needed. If two seem to apply, pick the one that tells the team what to do next.
  • 🔁
    Replace, do not stack.If a lead was tagged warm last week and today they say they have bought elsewhere, remove warm and tag cold. An old tag left behind sends them the wrong messages.
  • 📝
    Every call gets a note.Who they are, what they want, what was agreed, what is next. Plain words.
  • 💬
    "Currently in conversation" is automatic.It goes on the moment a lead replies to us, and comes off 24 hours after one of us replies back. It tells the automation to stay quiet while a human is talking. If you are mid conversation and you cannot see it, add it yourself.
  • 🙋
    "Human follow up needed" is for anything that does not fit.An unusual request, a question you could not answer, a complaint. This tag is what gets a person to look at it.
  • 👀
    Check the tag held.GHL sometimes drops a change. Close the contact and reopen it before you move on.

In short

  • Green system tags stay as they are
  • One tag per call, replace rather than stack
  • A note every time, in plain words
  • Currently in conversation goes on and off by itself; add it only if it is missing mid chat
  • Human follow up needed is the catch all
  • Reopen the contact to check the tag held

Calendars and bookings: the one thing that must be exact

A booking is not a diary entry. It is the trigger. The moment a lead is booked into Book a Call Back, the system moves them to Call Booked, confirms with them, sends reminders, and tells the closer. Booked into any other calendar, none of that happens and the lead thinks we forgot them.

Everything points at Book a Call BackThe pipeline, the automation and the AI assistant all watch this one calendar. If a booking is made anywhere else, nothing fires.

To begin with

Book every call into Book a Call Back only. Once you have a feel for how warm different callers are, and how our sales process runs, we will open up the choice of who a call goes to.

Every booking, and every contact, must be assigned to Liam James. If assignment can be automated so you never have to remember it, it will be, and this line will be removed.

In short

  • Calls: Book a Call Back. Visits: showroom calendar, name to confirm
  • Every booking and every contact assigned to Liam James

Section 6

Working in GHL, click by click

Only the parts you will actually use. Screenshots will be dropped into the marked slots. Each block is one task, start to finish.

🔍 Find the lead and open the contact

  1. Open the alert, or go to Contacts in the left menuNew Wave 2 leads carry the tag wave 2 facebook/meta lead. Filter by that tag to see only them.
  2. Click the name to open the contact recordCheck the assigned user shows Liam James. If it does not, set it now.
Screenshot slot: Contacts list filtered by the Wave 2 tag, with the assigned user field circled

📞 Make the call from GHL

  1. Click the phone icon on the contact recordThe call goes through GHL and is recorded. This is what makes the call visible to the system.
  2. Let the call end naturally, even a no answerHanging up early on a ringing line can stop GHL logging it as a completed attempt.
Screenshot slot: Contact record with the phone icon and the call panel open

📝 Log the note and the tag

  1. In the Notes panel, write what happenedWho, what they want, what was agreed, what is next.
  2. In Tags, remove any of your old tags and add the one that fits this callOne tag. Green system tags stay.
  3. Close the contact, reopen it, check note and tag are thereSaved is not the same as held.
Screenshot slot: Notes panel and Tags field on a contact, with a finished example note

📅 Book a call or a visit

  1. On the contact, open Appointments and click BookChoose Book a Call Back for a call. Check the name before you pick a slot.
  2. Pick the slot, confirm the contact details, saveThen open the contact's Appointments again and check it is listed against Book a Call Back.
Screenshot slot: Booking panel with the calendar dropdown open and Book a Call Back highlighted

🔕 Do Not Disturb

DND stops all automated messages to a contact. Use it when someone asks us to stop contacting them, or when a person is now handling them directly and the automated messages would get in the way.

  1. On the contact record, find the DND toggle at the top of the contact detailsYou can switch off all channels, or just one, such as SMS.
  2. Switch it on, then add a note saying why and whenWithout the note nobody will know why the messages stopped.
Screenshot slot: DND toggle on a contact, with the channel options showing

✋ Move a lead in the pipeline by hand

  1. Go to Opportunities in the left menu and pick the Wave 2 Facebook/Meta Ad pipelineEach card is a lead. Columns are the stages from Section 2.
  2. Drag the card to the correct stage, then add a note on the contact saying whyOnly when the stage is plainly wrong. See the rule in Section 2.
Screenshot slot: Pipeline board view with a card mid drag

💬 Send a message using a template

Most messages to leads are automated. If you ever need to send one yourself, WhatsApp has a rule: outside 24 hours of the customer's last message, only an approved template can be sent. A typed message in that window silently fails. Templates are the safe route.

  1. Open Conversations, select the contact, choose WhatsApp as the channelIf it is more than 24 hours since they last wrote to us, use a template.
  2. Click the template icon, pick the one that fits, fill any blanks, sendThen check the message shows as delivered, not just sent.
Screenshot slot: Conversation window with the template picker open
For discussion with LiamDoes AMP ever message a lead themselves, or only call? If only call, this block can be cut and the risk of a failed typed message goes away.

In short

  • Filter contacts by the Wave 2 tag, check assigned user is Liam James
  • Call from inside GHL, let it end naturally
  • Note, one tag, reopen to check
  • Book into Book a Call Back, check it landed
  • DND with a note saying why
  • WhatsApp outside 24 hours is templates only
