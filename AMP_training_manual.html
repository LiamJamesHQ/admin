Wave 2 leads, and the part you play in every one of them

From early September, Facebook and Meta ads start sending new enquiries into the system. The exact go-live day will be confirmed. You are the first human voice they hear. This manual covers what we need from you on each call, how the system reacts to what you log, and how to work the parts of GHL you will touch.

Section 1

🎯What we need from you, on one page

You already answer our phones and qualify callers every day. Wave 2 changes one thing: these leads come in through the system, and the system reacts to what you record. Four things, in this order, every time.

  • 📧
    Your day starts with the heads-up emailIt lands before calls begin with the day's lead count, so you know what is coming.
  • 📞
    Take the bridged calls from 10:30, Monday to FridayYou do not dial. The system rings you and connects you to the lead, and every call is logged automatically because of it.
  • 👀
    After the call, check the record landedThe AI writes the outcome, note and tags from the transcript. Read it, and add anything it could not hear. Section 4 covers the tags that are still yours.
  • 📅

Why it matters
Once you have logged the call, automation takes over: follow up messages, reminders, and alerts to the team all fire off what you recorded. If the record is wrong or missing, the customer gets the wrong message or none at all.

Moving a lead by hand

You can drag a lead between stages, and there will be moments when you should. Two things to know first: a hand move can be overwritten a few minutes later when the automation writes its own outcome, and some stages fire messages when a lead lands in them. So the rule is: log the outcome and let the system move it, and only move by hand when the stage is plainly wrong and you have already left a note saying why.

Section 3

🔀Four outcomes, and what each one sets off

Every completed call lands in one of these. Your note and tag decide which. Here is what happens next in each case, so you can tell a caller what to expect.

🔥Connected, warm

You doBook them into the call back calendar there and then if they will take a slot. If not, note and tag warm.
ThenIf booked: confirmation, reminders and closer notification fire. If not booked: a WhatsApp with the invitation to visit the workshop, then a gentle check in sequence from Marta over the following days.
NeverGive a price or an estimate. The closer does that on the booked call.

❄️Connected, cold

You doNote the reason they are not interested. Tag cold.
ThenOne light cross sell message showing the wider range, then the system leaves them alone.
NeverPush. A clear "no thanks" is a fine outcome and a clean record.

📵No answer or voicemail

You doLet the call finish so GHL records it. Note "no answer" or "voicemail left". Do not call again yourself yet.
ThenThe next working day at lunchtime the system rings you again with a fresh bridged call to them. If that also goes unanswered, a timed chase of messages runs over the following days. If they reply, a task lands in your queue to call again.
NeverRing three times in a row. The chase is designed so one attempt is enough from you.

🤔Connected, unclear

You doWrite the fullest note of the four. What they asked, what you could not answer, what they want next. Tag human follow up needed.
ThenIf nothing has moved in 48 hours, an alert goes to Nikki, who reads your note and routes it.
NeverLeave it with no note. A blank unclear is the one outcome the system cannot recover.
How the system reads your call
After every call, an automation reads the call and your note and writes an outcome. A clear note in plain words ("wants a 6 person sauna for a garden, asked about delivery to Devon, happy to book Thursday") makes that reading accurate. A one word note makes it guess.

Section 4

🏷️Tags and notes: the job that makes everything else work

Most tags are written by the system and the AI off your call. Your job is lighter but just as important: add the two tags that are yours when they apply, and correct anything that no longer fits. Clean tags are the difference between a customer getting the right message and the wrong one.

Two kinds of tag

Some tags are placed by the system. Never add, remove or edit these, even if one looks wrong. Other tags are yours to manage.

wave 2 facebook/meta lead wave 2 no answer chase started no answer chase exhausted wave 2 call booked closer call outcome is ...

System tags, shaded green. Leave them alone.

🔥warm ❄️cold 📵no answer 💬currently in conversation 🙋human follow up needed

Your tags, honey outline. Exact spellings to be confirmed, see Open decisions.

The rules

  • 1️⃣
    One clean status per contact. When you add or correct a tag, leave the one that tells the team what to do next, not a pile of history.
  • 🔁
    Replace, do not stack. If a lead was tagged warm last week and today they say they have bought elsewhere, remove warm and tag cold. An old tag left behind sends them the wrong messages.
  • 📝
    Every call gets a note, and the AI writes it. Check it landed, and add anything it could not hear: side details, anything agreed off the recording.
  • 💬
    "Currently in conversation" goes on automatically the moment a lead replies to us, and comes off 24 hours after one of us replies back. It tells the automation to stay quiet while a human is talking. If you are mid conversation and you cannot see it, add it yourself.
  • 🙋
    "Human follow up needed" is for anything that does not fit. An unusual request, a question you could not answer, a complaint. This tag is what gets a person to look at it.
  • 👀
    Check the tag saved. GHL sometimes drops a change. Close the contact and reopen it before you move on.

🔕Do Not Disturb

DND stops all automated messages to a contact. Use it when someone asks us to stop contacting them, or when a person is now handling them directly and the automated messages would get in the way.

  1. On the contact record, find the DND toggle at the top of the contact detailsYou can switch off all channels, or just one, such as SMS.
  2. Switch it on, then add a note saying why and whenWithout the note nobody will know why the messages stopped.

💬Send a message using a template

Most messages to leads are automated. If you ever need to send one yourself, WhatsApp has a rule: outside 24 hours of the customer's last message, only an approved template can be sent. A typed message in that window silently fails. Templates are the safe route.

  1. Open Conversations, select the contact, choose WhatsApp as the channelIf it is more than 24 hours since they last wrote to us, you must use a template.
  2. Click the template icon, pick the one that fits, fill any blanks, sendThen check the message shows as delivered, not just sent.

✅Tasks assigned to you

When a chased lead needs a second human call, the system creates a task against your name. Tasks are the queue of calls the system could not make for you.

  1. Open Tasks in the left menu, filter to your nameEach task names the contact and what it wants, usually a call-again on a lead who did not answer.
  2. Open the contact from the task, make the call, check the recordAs always.
  3. Mark the task completeAn open task means an uncalled lead, so the list should be empty by the end of each day.
