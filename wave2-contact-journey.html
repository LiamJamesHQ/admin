You already answer our phones and qualify callers every day. Wave 2 changes one thing: these leads come in through the system, and the system reacts to every call. Four things, in this order, every time.

📧 Your day starts with the heads-up email

📞 Take the bridged calls, 10:30 to 17:00, Monday to Friday

👀 After the call, check the record landed

📅 Book into the right calendar
Calls with the closer into Book a Call Back, workshop visits into Showroom Bookings. Section 5.
WHY IT MATTERS
The AI logs every call from the transcript, and the automation fires off that record: follow up messages, reminders, alerts to the team. Your check after each call is the safety net. If the AI misread something and nobody corrects it, the customer gets the wrong message, or none at all.

MOVING A LEAD BY HAND
You can drag a lead between stages, and there will be moments when you should. Two things to know first: a hand move can be overwritten a few minutes later when the automation writes its own outcome, and some stages fire messages when a lead lands in them. So the rule is: let the system move it, and only move by hand when the stage is plainly wrong and you have already left a note saying why.

WHAT THE AI WROTE
The automation reads the transcript. Your job is to make sure what it wrote is true. 👀

Every completed call lands in one of these. The AI reads the call and writes the outcome; your check makes it right. Here is what happens next in each case, so you can tell a caller what to expect.

🔥 Connected, warm
YOU DO
Book them into Book a Call Back there and then if they will take a slot. If not, check the record shows warm.
THEN
If booked: confirmation, reminders and closer notification fire. If not booked: a WhatsApp with the invitation to visit the workshop, then a gentle check in sequence from Marta over the following days.
NEVER
Give a price or an estimate. The closer does that on the booked call.
❄️ Connected, cold
YOU DO
Add the reason they are not interested to the record. Check the tag shows cold.
THEN
One cross sell message showing the wider range, then two well spaced check-ins at 8 and 12 weeks. A reply at any point warms them back up and a person picks it up.
NEVER
Push, and never promise silence. A clear "no thanks" is a fine outcome, but the later check-ins will still go out unless they ask us to stop contacting them altogether. That is what DND is for, Section 6.
📵 No answer or voicemail
YOU DO
Let the call finish so GHL records it. Do not call again yourself.
THEN
The next working day at lunchtime the system bridges you a fresh call to them. If that also goes unanswered, a timed chase of messages runs over the following days. If they reply, a task lands in your queue to call again, Section 6.
NEVER
Ring three times in a row. The retry and the chase are designed so one attempt at a time is enough from you.
🤔 Connected, unclear
YOU DO
Write the fullest note of the four, on top of whatever the AI wrote. What they asked, what you could not answer, what they want next. Tag human follow up needed.
THEN
If nothing has moved in 48 hours, an alert goes to the team, who read your note and route it.
NEVER
Leave it with no note. A blank unclear is the one outcome the system cannot recover.
HOW THE SYSTEM READS YOUR CALL
After every call, the recording is transcribed and an AI classifies the conversation, then writes the outcome, the note and the tags. A clear, natural conversation makes that reading accurate. Your added note catches what the transcript cannot carry: anything agreed off the call, background the caller gave in passing, or a correction where the AI plainly misheard.

Most tags are written by the system and the AI off your call. Your job is lighter but just as important: add the two tags that are yours when they apply, and correct anything that no longer fits. Clean tags are the difference between a customer getting the right message and the wrong one.

TWO KINDS OF TAG
Some tags are placed by the system. Never add, remove or edit these, even if one looks wrong. Other tags are yours to manage.

THE TRIGGER
A booking is not a diary entry. It is the moment the whole system wakes up. 📅

3
Mark the task complete

🔕 DO NOT DISTURB
DND stops all automated messages to a contact. Use it when someone asks us to stop contacting them, or when a person is now handling them directly and the automated messages would get in the way.
1
On the contact record, find the DND toggle at the top of the contact details
You can switch off all channels, or just one, such as SMS.
2
Switch it on, then add a note saying why and when
Without the note nobody will know why the messages stopped.

💬 SEND A MESSAGE USING A TEMPLATE
Most messages to leads are automated. If you ever need to send one yourself, WhatsApp has a rule: outside 24 hours of the customer's last message, only an approved template can be sent. A typed message in that window silently fails. Templates are the safe route.
1
Open Conversations, select the contact, choose WhatsApp as the channel
If it is more than 24 hours since they last wrote to us, you must use a template.
2
Click the template icon, pick the one that fits, fill any blanks, send
Then check the message shows as delivered, not just sent.
