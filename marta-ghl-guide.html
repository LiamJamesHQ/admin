A smart list is a saved search that never goes stale. You set the filter once, and the list quietly keeps itself up to date as contacts come and go. Build one for anything you check more than once a week.

1
👤 Open Contacts
You start from the full list of everyone.
2
🔍 Click More Filters
A panel opens on the right with every filter available.
3
🎯 Pick your filter and value
For example Assigned, then leave it as empty. The list narrows as you go.
4
💾 Save as smart list and name it
It appears as a tab at the top of Contacts from then on.

Every contact has an Owner field, the person at BSUK responsible for them. When it is empty, nobody is responsible, and that is exactly how a warm lead goes cold without anyone noticing.

🔔 Notified
The owner gets the notifications about that contact, so the right person hears when something happens.
📊 Credited
Calls and results are credited to the owner, so we can see who is handling what.
🙌 Accountable
One name means one person who will answer the question whatever happened with this customer.

A tag is a label on a contact. They look harmless, but at BSUK the tags are the wiring: automations read them to decide who gets which message, and when.

Tags placed and read by the system, shaded green. Leave them alone. 🟢

wave 2 facebook/meta lead wave 2 call booked amp call outcome is 'warm' closer call outcome is 'confirmed'

You will see many like these on Wave 2 contacts. They are how the automation steers.

Tags people manage, honey outline. You may be asked to add one of these. 🖐️

💬 currently in conversation 🙋 human follow up needed

Exact spellings for these are being confirmed with Liam. Until then, add tags only when Nikki asks.

1
🚫 Never delete a tag
The system never removes tags itself, and an automation may be waiting to read that one. Deleting it can silently stop a customer's messages.
2
✍️ Never rename a tag
Automations match the exact spelling. One changed letter and the workflow simply stops finding anyone.
3
✅ Adding is fine, when asked
If Nikki or a checklist tells you to add a specific tag, that is safe. Type it exactly, all lowercase.
WHY SO STRICT
Because tag mistakes are invisible. Nothing turns red, no error appears. A customer just quietly stops hearing from us, and nobody finds out for weeks. Treat tags as read-only unless told otherwise and that can never happen on your watch. 👀
