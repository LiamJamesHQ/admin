Section 02

Pipelines

Done

Every live enquiry sits on a board as a card. The column it sits in is the stage it has reached.

Worth knowing

The columns are still being tidied, so a card is not always in the right stage yet. When in doubt, trust what the contact record tells you over the column it happens to sit in.

What to spot
  • The pipeline dropdown top left. It switches between Facebook/Meta Ads, Google Ads and WIX Forms. Flick through them.
  • A card carries source, email, phone and current stage at a glance.
  • Open one card to see the full opportunity behind it.

Section 03

Your calendar

Done

Your booked sales calls land here. This is where you check your day.

What to spot
  • Manage view on the right, then under Calendars tick only "Ollie Jones Sales Calls" so you see just your own.
  • A booked call shows as a block on the day. Click it to see the contact and time.
  • New, top right, is how you add an appointment yourself. Do one together.

Section 04

Contacts and your lists

Done

Every enquiry is a contact record. Two smart lists are set up to hand you the ones that need working.

Your two lists

"Never contacted" is leads nobody has reached yet. "2 weeks" is leads whose last activity was a fortnight ago. Work whichever you like first, 2 weeks is probably the better start. Ignore the tags on a record for now and just read the conversation yourself.

What to spot
  • The search bar finds anyone by name, email or phone.
  • On an open record, the Owner field top left and the Tags row just below it.
  • Open both smart lists so he knows where his daily work comes from.

Section 05

Conversations and notes

Done

Two tabs on a record hold your two-minute read before any call. Know which does what.

The two tabs

Conversations holds the actual messages and the call recordings, each with a View Transcript link. Notes holds the tidy written summary our automation writes after every call: Outcome, Product discussed, Objections, Next touch. Recording under Conversations, summary under Notes.

What to spot
  • The Conversations tab, with call recordings you can play and a View Transcript link under each.
  • The Notes tab, with the CALL SUMMARY the automation writes on its own. He creates nothing here.
  • Open the two study records and listen to their recordings in full.

Section 06

Making calls

Done

Calls go out from inside GHL, so every one is logged and recorded against the contact.

What to spot
  • The action icons top right of the conversation, the phone icon starts a call.
  • After a call ends, the recording appears right in the thread as "Call completed".
  • The LeadConnector mobile app gives the same dialler when away from the desk.

Section 08

Your inbox

Done

The record you just assigned to yourself now shows here, in your own inbox.

What to spot
  • The person icon on the left of the inbox, set it to "Assigned to me".
  • Find the contact he just assigned to himself sitting in the list.
  • Reply from the box at the bottom, picking the channel the lead last used. Always reply here, not from his own phone.

Section 09

Proposals

Done

The full estimate documents we send when a deal is worth it. Have a look so you know what good looks like.

When this goes out

A proposal at this level is only sent when there is a serious buyer with a complex order. It is not for routine enquiries. Most leads never need one.

What to spot
  • Open both example proposals and take in the level of detail.
  • Open the estimate builder, that is how one gets made.
